## Decisions (one per item of your list)

**1. Families.** Transitions: `kalman`'s only (random walk with `Q D²` charged per observation, per-slot reversion); linear, additive Gaussian. So the EKF/UKF prediction step is exact and 496's smoother applies unchanged. Observations: scalar, through one index `η = z'β`: gaussian identity (today's kalman); gaussian with log/logit mean (the saturating logistic and exponential curves; `obs_var` required); `family.rs`'s poisson-log, binomial-logit, gamma-log/inverse, tweedie-log/power; Student t for the particle filter only. Power law (log link, `ln x` features) and Michaelis-Menten (inverse link, `1/s` feature) need no family. Refused: arbitrary expressions (a serialized spec must be deterministic, no evaluator or closure in the core), range-bearing (a joint two-coordinate observation with a wrapped angle; the bank's targets are independent scalars), multi-index observations. The particle filter's engine is generic over an internal trait, so a named nonlinear transition (stochastic volatility next) is one enum variant later.

**2. EKF Jacobian.** Closed form only: `H = (dμ/dη)(η̃) z'` at the clipped `η̃` (`family.rs`'s `ETA_CLIP = 30`), linearized at the clipped value and never through the clip's derivative (study §2). No finite differences: every offered pair has a closed form, and a difference step is a tuned constant whose rounding differs by platform. Update = kalman's own scalar step with `z → m z`, `R = φV/w`. Both of kalman's covariance forms (standard, Carlson), not filterpy's `4k³` Joseph matrix product: the two differ only on clip-reaching streams, where the EKF has already lost track.

**3. UKF points.** Merwe scaled, filterpy's names. `β = 2`, `κ = 0` from Wan and van der Merwe (2000); **`α = 1` in place of their `1e-3`**: at `1e-3` the centre weight is about `-1e6` (six digits lost, study D4), and a small α turns the UKF into a second-order EKF, giving up the spread that kept it on track (EKF lost 15 of 600 streams, UKF 0). Prior exact (`P⁻ = ΦPΦ + Q`); `P` symmetrized each update; a failed Cholesky takes that row's EKF step and is flagged (no jitter). **Derived, not run:** on a one-index observation the full rule's fourth moment in `η` is `Σ(z'L_i)⁴/(k+λ)`, between `s⁴` and `k s⁴` at (1,2,0), depending on the feature order and the anchor. Worked: `E[e^η]` at `s = 0.5` is off by +4.7% at k = 20, and correct to 1e-4 at `k+λ = 3`. A scalar-index Gauss-Hermite rule (3 points on `η`, `C = P⁻z·Cov(η,h)/s²`) is `O(k²)`, independent of the coordinates, and exactly filterpy's UKF with `JulierSigmaPoints(k, 3−k)` and a square root aligned on `P⁻z`. Proposed: build both, measure on the study's second oracle, and default to the closer one.

**4. Particle resampling.** Resample when `ESS < τN` (`resample_ess`, default 0.5): a condition on the cloud, never a cadence or a row count. Systematic by default; stratified, residual and multinomial too (filterpy's four). Sequential cumsum, last entry set to 1 (filterpy's systematic/stratified can raise there; ours clamps). **Determinism:** each observing row `n` draws from SplitMix64 (already in core) started at `mix(key, n)`. `n` (u64 per target) and `key = mix(seed, stable hash of group key)` (u64 per group) live in the state. A row's draw count never shifts later rows, so a chunk boundary or a save and load cannot move a draw. Normals come from AS241 inverse CDF (one uniform each, `ln`/`sqrt` only in the tails), sums run in particle order, and a cloud is never split across threads. A zero-weight or null row is predicted from the stream row `n` will use and consumes nothing (rule 9). q = 0 is refused (no rejuvenation). The intercept gets a proper prior, because a diffuse intercept is improper after one Poisson zero. `standardize` is refused in v1. Collapse shows in the `ess` output; there is no reset and no jitter.

**5. State.** EKF/UKF: **no new state field**. The kalman config gets new fields appended at the end, `serde(default)` and written only when they differ from the default, so no existing byte moves: `family`, `link`, `var_power`, `dispersion` φ (fixed), `filter` (linear/ekf/ukf), `alpha`/`beta`/`kappa`, `ukf_noise`. Under a non-Gaussian family: `P` absolute, φ fixed, `obs_var` refused, `q` explicit, and `coef_half_life` refused (its matching assumes information `zz'/σ²`, while a GLM row's is `Wzz'/φ`). Particle: **new `ModelState::Particle` appended last**, within unreleased schema 72 (no bump; you regenerate the fixtures at the merge). Persisted bytes are **`8(k+1)` per particle per target** (k coefficients plus one log-weight), i.e. `8TN(k+1) + 24T + 8` per group. Resampling scratch is `8k+4` per particle and is not persisted. At N = 1,000: 24 KB at k = 2, 48 KB at k = 5, 168 KB at k = 20. The cap is 537's 256 KB per group, `N(k+1)T ≤ 32,768`, checked in `validate`. Fixture cases cover each form: unobserved (empty), weighted, just resampled, two targets with one observed, each family, each scheme; plus four kalman ekf/ukf configurations.

**6. Oracles.** The EKF is held to filterpy `ExtendedKalmanFilter` and the UKF to `UnscentedKalmanFilter`. filterpy 1.4.5 is already in the dev group; **no bump**: the UKF test hands the prior over each row (x, P, sigmas_f from its own points_fn), so 1.4.5's lack of a redraw after predict does not matter. Tolerances from study §10: 1e-12 rel on unclipped streams; 10× the filter's own 1-ulp band on clipped ones; 2e-14/5e-14 for the UKF with the prior handed over; 1e-13 against `KalmanFilter` on the linear case. Particle filter:
- (i) filterpy's four resamplers, index for index, on the same weights and uniforms. The test patches `filterpy.monte_carlo.resampling.random` and calls the Rust resampler through an internal extension function.
- (ii) The linear Gaussian case against filterpy `KalmanFilter`. Let `e = (m̂ − b)/sqrt(P/N)`, which tends to `N(0, ρ)`, `ρ ≥ 1` (Chopin 2004). Over R seeds and M = rows·k cells: bias `|ē| ≤ t_{R−1}(1−α/2M)·s/√R` (Bonferroni, α = 1e-3), efficiency `s² ≤ ρ_max`, and for a single run (essentials) `|e| ≤ sqrt(ρ_max)·Φ⁻¹(1−α/2M)`. `ρ_max` is measured in part 2 and only tightens. At N = 1,000 the Monte Carlo sd is 0.03 to 0.1 posterior sd for ρ between 1 and 10.
- (iii) Gordon, Salmond and Smith (1993), the univariate nonstationary growth model (`x/2 + 25x/(1+x²) + 8cos(1.2t) + N(0,10)`, `y = x²/20 + N(0,1)`), whose posterior is bimodal. It runs through a test-only implementation of the engine's internal trait (not a product family), with the forcing passed as an input so the model reads no clock. **The reference values come from a 1-D point-mass grid filter on the test's own seeded stream**, not from the paper, whose figures come from its own draws. The grid is checked by refinement and by a linear variant against the Kalman filter. It holds the mean and `P(x>0)`; the prior variance is checked against the paper before it is cited.
- (iv) The GLM families against the study's grid at k = 2; Student t against a grid and `scipy.stats.t.logpdf`.

**7. Cost and the smoother.**
- Per observing row: the EKF is **`O(k²)`**, about 5k², not `O(k³)`. `O(k³)` only applies to the Joseph matrix product, or Carlson's form under process noise. The UKF full rule is about `k³/3 + 13k²` with `2k+1` link calls (the scalar-index rule about 6k² with 3). The PF costs `N·(k normals + 3k flops + 1 link + 1 log-lik)`, plus `O(Nk)` per resampling: about 1e5 flops at N = 1,000, k = 5, two to three orders above kalman (derived).
- Smoother: the transition is linear, so 496's RTS ring over `(b, P, P⁻, F)` is exactly the extended and unscented RTS (Särkkä 2008: with linear dynamics the cross-covariance is `PΦ'`). 497 refuses `smooth_lag` with a nonlinear filter until 496 merges; a follow-up lifts it with one filterpy `rts_smoother` test.
- No particle smoother: ancestor paths take `8LNk` bytes (2 MB at L = 50, N = 1,000, k = 5, eight times the cap) and degenerate.

**Platforms.** Every `exp`/`ln`/`lgamma` in the three filters comes from the `libm` crate, as in garch: it is already linked, so nothing new is linked, and the state is bit-exact on every OS. This is required for the PF, because a resampling index is a step function of the weights and no tolerance is tiny there. It is proposed for the EKF/UKF, because clipped streams amplify one ulp to 1e-10. `family.rs` becomes generic over the math provider, so glm's outputs do not move.
